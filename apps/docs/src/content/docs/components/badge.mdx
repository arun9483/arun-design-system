## When to use

- The status of an item in a list or table: "Paid", "Overdue"
- A category or stage: "Beta", "New"
- A count next to a label

Not for something people click or remove — use a Chip or a Button.

**Choosing between small labels:**

| Component                 | Use it for                                         | For example                          |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Badge**                 | The status or category of something, at a glance   | "Paid", "Overdue", "Beta"            |
| [Chip](/components/chip/) | Tags, filters and metadata, often several in a row | "React", "Remote", an applied filter |
| [Kbd](/components/kbd/)   | A key on the keyboard, in text or a tooltip        | Press ⌘ K to search                  |

## Domain vocabulary belongs to your app

The design system ships only generic tones. Anything narrower — an article's difficulty, an
order's status, a build result — is your vocabulary, and mapping it at the call site keeps it
type-checked and keeps the library reusable.

## Notes

- Tones are backed by the `--color-status-*` semantic tokens, so they move with the brand.
  Override `--badge-success-bg` to restyle only success badges.
- `neutral` adds no tone class, so `.badge` alone is the neutral appearance.
