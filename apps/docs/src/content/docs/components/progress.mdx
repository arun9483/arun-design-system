`Progress` is a native `<progress>`. Give it `value` and `max` to show how far along a task is.
Leave out `value` and it is indeterminate: a short bar sweeps across while you wait. The sweep
stops under reduced motion.

Name it with a `<label>` or `aria-label`, and put the percentage in the text if it matters.

## Notes

- To show how much of a range is used, such as disk space or password strength, use a
  [Meter](/components/meter/).
- Restyle with `--progress-height`, `--progress-track-color` and `--progress-bar-color`.
