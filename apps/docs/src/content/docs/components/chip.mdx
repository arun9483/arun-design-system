## Semantics matter more than styling

A chip is a shape, not an element. In a list it must be an `li`; when it is clickable it must
be a real `button`, or it is invisible to keyboards and screen readers. `render` supplies the
styling while you keep the right element.

## Notes

- `<Chip render={<button />}>` needs `type="button"` on the element, or it defaults to
  `type="submit"` and will submit an enclosing form.
- `Badge` builds on `Chip`'s shape — a badge carries both `chip` and `badge` classes.
