## When to use

- A person’s picture next to their name, in comments, lists and headers
- Initials or a placeholder when there is no picture, or it fails to load
- A stack of people on a shared item

Not for logos or general images — use a plain `<img>`.

`Avatar` shows a picture once it has loaded, and initials or an icon until then. If the picture
fails, or there is none, the initials stay. A broken image never shows.

```tsx
<Avatar.Root>
  <Avatar.Image src={user.photo} alt={user.name} />
  <Avatar.Fallback delay={300}>{initials}</Avatar.Fallback>
</Avatar.Root>
```

- `delay` holds the initials back briefly, so a picture that loads fast doesn't flash them first.
- Initials are read out letter by letter. Give the Root `role="img"` and an `aria-label` with
  the name, and mark the initials `aria-hidden`. Or leave them be when the name is shown beside
  the avatar.
- The fallback can hold anything: initials, an icon, or a placeholder picture, as the third
  avatar in the example shows. Make an image fill it with `inline-size` and `block-size` of
  `100%`; the circle clips it. Use a local or inline image for it, so the fallback itself can't
  fail to load.
- Size it with `--avatar-size`.
