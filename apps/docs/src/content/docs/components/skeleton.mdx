`Skeleton` is a shape that stands in for content while it loads. It is hidden from screen
readers. Mark the region it fills with `aria-busy="true"` instead, and remove that once the
content arrives.

Its size and shape are yours, set with `style` or a class. It is one line of text high by
default. The shine stops under reduced motion.
