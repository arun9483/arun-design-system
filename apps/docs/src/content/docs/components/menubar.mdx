## When to use

- An application's menus along the top of an editor or a tool: File, Edit, View
- A side rail of menus in a workspace

Not for a single "More actions ⋯" button, which is a [Menu](/components/menu/), nor for a row of
buttons, which is a [Toolbar](/components/toolbar/).

**Choosing between menus and rows of controls:**

| Component                       | Use it for                                               | For example                         |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Menubar**                     | Several menus side by side, moved between as one bar     | An editor's File / Edit / View      |
| [Menu](/components/menu/)       | One list of actions or settings opened from a button     | "More actions ⋯" on a row or a card |
| [Toolbar](/components/toolbar/) | A row of buttons, toggles and fields acting on one thing | An editor's formatting bar          |

`Menubar` follows the [WAI-ARIA menubar pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/menubar/).
The bar is `role="menubar"` and one Tab stop; each `Menubar.Trigger` is one of its items.

- ← and → move along the bar, wrapping; Home and End go to its ends. Typing moves to the Trigger
  whose text starts with what was typed.
- ↓, Enter and Space open a menu at its first item; ↑ opens it at its last.
- Inside an open menu, → and ← move to the next and previous menu and open it. A
  `SubmenuTrigger`'s → opens its submenu first, and inside a submenu ← closes it first.
- While a menu is open, the pointer reaching another Trigger opens that menu instead.
- Esc, a click outside and Tab close the menu, as for [Menu](/components/menu/).

In a right-to-left bar ← and → swap. Disabled Triggers are skipped, unless the Root sets
`focusableWhenDisabled`. Name the bar with `aria-label`.

The parts are Menu's, under the bar's name: `Menubar.Menu` holds one menu's state, as
`Menu.Root` does, and `Menubar.Popup`, `Menubar.Item`, `Menubar.CheckboxItem`,
`Menubar.RadioGroup`, `Menubar.RadioItem`, `Menubar.ItemIndicator`, `Menubar.Group`,
`Menubar.GroupLabel`, `Menubar.SubmenuRoot` and `Menubar.SubmenuTrigger` work as on
[Menu](/components/menu/).

## Submenus

Menus nest to any depth with `Menubar.SubmenuRoot` and `Menubar.SubmenuTrigger`, as on
[Menu](/components/menu/#submenus): → (or resting the pointer on it) opens a submenu, ← closes it,
and Esc closes one level at a time. → on an item that opens nothing moves along the bar to the next
menu, closing every level.

## Vertical

With `orientation="vertical"`, ↑ and ↓ move along the bar, → opens a menu beside it, and ← closes
it back to its Trigger. The menus open to the right. In a right-to-left page, pass
`side="left"` to each `Menubar.Popup`; `side` stays physical, as on every popup.
