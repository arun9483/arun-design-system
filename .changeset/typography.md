Add `Heading`, `Paragraph` and `Text`. `Heading` renders `<h1>`–`<h6>` from `level`, with `size` setting the look apart from the rank. `Paragraph` is a `<p>` capped at a readable line length. `Text` is a `<span>` with `size`, `color` and `weight`. New types: `HeadingLevel`, `TextSize`, `TextColor` and `TextWeight`, plus the `font-weight-normal` utility. Tokens: `--heading-font-family`, `--heading-font-weight`, `--heading-letter-spacing`, `--heading-color` and `--paragraph-max-inline-size`.
